The files in this folder are derived from the data and translations of Cataclysm: Dark Days
Ahead (https://github.com/CleverRaven/Cataclysm-DDA), by its contributors.

They are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported License, like
Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead itself: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

Each <version>/ folder records the CDDA commit and the translation release it was generated
from (catalog.json "meta", index.json).

builtin-kinds.json is generated by tools/classify/builtin-kinds.mjs, using rules ported from
cataclysm-3d (https://github.com/Risaro/cataclysm-3d, a CDDA fork, CC BY-SA 3.0).

Chinese names marked as unofficial ("generated" in names.zh_CN.json) come from
tools/editor/names/zh_CN.json, also under CC BY-SA 3.0.
